# Authoring Review Checklist

- Does the document answer one primary question?
- Is the primary reader clear?
- Are current fact, accepted decision, target design and future plan separated?
- Are repeated explanations replaced by canonical links?
- Are tables and visuals used where they improve information density?
- Does every local image resolve inside the same document bundle?
- Does every image have an immediate AI-readable semantic mirror?
- Does the mirror preserve nodes, relations, boundaries, states and conditions?
- Are evidence, uncertainty, non-goals and limitations explicit?
- Are headings portable to Git and Feishu?
- Are secrets, personal data and unsupported claims absent?
- Is the result a candidate pending Planner/user Review rather than an unauthorized write?
